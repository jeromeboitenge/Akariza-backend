1. ✅ Real-Time Dashboard
   GET /analytics/dashboard
   
   Returns:
   • Today's sales & transactions
   • Month & year totals
   • Top 5 products today
   • Low stock count
   • Recent 5 sales
   • Cashier performance
   • Pending payments

2. ✅ Sales Trends Analysis
   GET /analytics/sales-trends?days=7&period=daily
   
   Features:
   • Daily/weekly/monthly trends
   • Configurable time period
   • Sales amount & transaction count
   • Identify peak days

3. ✅ Top Products Report
   GET /analytics/top-products?limit=10&days=30
   
   Shows:
   • Best selling products
   • Quantity sold
   • Revenue generated
   • Transaction count
   • Average price

4. ✅ Smart Stock Alerts
   GET /analytics/low-stock-alerts
   
   Intelligence:
   • Status: OUT_OF_STOCK, CRITICAL, LOW
   • Days until stockout prediction
   • Based on 30-day sales average
   • Automatic reorder suggestions

5. ✅ Revenue by Category
   GET /analytics/revenue-by-category?startDate=...&endDate=...
   
   Breakdown:
   • Revenue per category
   • Quantity sold
   • Transaction count
   • Sorted by revenue

6. ✅ Payment Method Analytics
   GET /analytics/payment-methods?startDate=...&endDate=...
   
   Tracks:
   • CASH, MOBILE, CARD breakdown
   • Total amount per method
   • Transaction count
   • Payment preferences
